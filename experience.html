About Me

I’m a recent Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Bucknell University with interests in GIS, software development, data analysis, and interactive systems. My work combines technical problem-solving with communication and design, particularly in projects involving data visualization, web applications, and geospatial analysis.
My experience includes development work in Python, C, R, Linux-based systems, Git, and ArcGIS Pro/ArcGIS Online. I’m especially interested in using GIS and software tools to improve how people interact with complex information. Alongside my technical background, I value collaboration, documentation, and designing systems that are both functional and accessible.
