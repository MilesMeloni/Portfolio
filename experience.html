Senior Thesis

During my Senior Thesis (2025 - 2026) I continued work I had begun as a Presidential Fellow the year prior. I collaborated with Bucknell University faculty and Laboratori Nazionali del Gran Sasso (LNGS) to design mathematical models to predict the behavior of fields within a time displacement chamber. This specialized detector type uses temporal data to glean a 3-dimensional map of particle interactions. My goal was to derive formulas relating voltages of charged components to the strain experienced by sensitive detector equipment. These mathematical models are now utilized by the organization to optimize detector function.
